# Capability 12: Psychopathology (DSM-5 vs. RDoC), Evidence-Based Therapies, and Stress-Coping Resilience

## R - Source Anchor (Reading)

- **Source Location**: Chapter 12 (Psychological Disorders, `Core Concepts 12.1-12.3`), Chapter 13 (Therapies for Psychological Disorders, `Core Concepts 13.1-13.4`), and Chapter 14 (From Stress to Health and Well-Being, `Core Concepts 14.1-14.4`).
- **Core Mechanism**:
  - **Biopsychosocial Psychopathology (`Ch 12`)**: Mental disorders arise from the **Diathesis-Stress interaction** (genetic/epigenetic/brain-hub vulnerability + environmental stressor) and require evaluating **4 Indicators of Abnormality** (`Distress, Maladaptiveness, Irrationality, Unpredictability/Unconventionality`) while avoiding **Rosenhan Diagnostic Labeling Depersonalization**.
  - **Modality-Matched Evidence-Based Therapy (`Ch 13`)**: Effective treatment combines the **Therapeutic Alliance** with disorder-matched **Psychological Therapies** (`Insight, Behavioral Exposure/Operant, CBT/REBT, Positive Psychotherapy`) and/or **Biomedical Therapies** (`Antipsychotics, Antidepressants, Mood Stabilizers, Anxiolytics, ECT/rTMS/DBS`).
  - **Stress Physiology, Appraisal, and Resilience (`Ch 14`)**: Stress activates the fast **SAM (`Adrenaline`)** and slow **HPA (`Cortisol`)** axes across **Selye's 3-Stage General Adaptation Syndrome (`GAS`)** (or **Taylor's `Tend-and-Befriend` oxytocin response**), suppressing immunity (**Psychoneuroimmunology**) when chronic. Resilience depends on **Lazarus's Primary/Secondary Cognitive Appraisal**, **Hardiness (`3Cs`)**, **Problem- vs. Emotion-Focused Coping**, **Zimbardo's Time Perspective Therapy (`TPT`)**, and the **4 Lifestyle Pillars** (`Social Support, Nutrition, Sleep/Meditation, Aerobic Exercise`).

## I - Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

### 1. The 4 Indicators of Abnormality & DSM-5 / NIMH RDoC Diagnostic Matrix (`Ch 12.2-12.12`)

- **4 Indicators of Abnormality (`12.2.1`)**: No single indicator suffices alone; clinicians evaluate the convergence of:
  1. **Distress** (persistent subjective emotional or physical pain)
  2. **Maladaptiveness** (impairment of work, school, self-care, or relationships)
  3. **Irrationality** (behavior/speech incomprehensible or out of touch with shared reality)
  4. **Unpredictability & Unconventionality** (erratic loss of control violating culturally appropriate norms)
- **DSM-5 vs. NIMH Research Domain Criteria (`RDoC`, `12.12`)**: The **DSM-5** classifies `~300+` disorders by descriptive symptom checklists (high inter-rater reliability, but overlapping comorbidities), whereas **NIMH RDoC** reconceptualizes disorders across underlying **genetic, epigenetic, neurotransmitter, and brain "hub" network dysfunctions** (`frontoparietal, default mode, limbic hubs`).

### 2. Psychotherapy Modalities & Ellis's REBT / Beck's CBT Restructuring (`Ch 13.3-13.5`)

- **3 Common Factors of All Effective Therapies (`13.1.2`)**: (1) **Therapeutic Alliance** (warm, empathetic, collaborative bond), (2) **Protected Setting**, (3) **Credible Explanatory Myth/Rationale & Ritual** restoring client self-efficacy (`Frank`).
- **Albert Ellis's REBT `ABCDE` Model & Aaron Beck's Cognitive Therapy (`13.3.4 & 13.5.1`)**:
  - **`A` (Activating Event)** -> **`B` (Belief / Irrational "Musts" & Catastrophizing, not `A` itself!)** -> **`C` (Emotional/Behavioral Consequence)** -> **`D` (Dispute the irrational belief empirically and logically)** -> **`E` (Effective new philosophy & behavior)**.
- **Martin Seligman's Positive Psychotherapy (`PPT`, `13.5.2`)**: Directly builds positive emotion, engagement (`signature strengths`), and meaning (`Three Good Things / Gratitude Visit`), reducing depressive symptoms alongside CBT.

### 3. Stress Physiology (`GAS`), Lazarus Appraisal, and Resilience Architecture (`Ch 14`)

1. **Dual Stress Pathways & Selye's 3-Stage GAS vs. Taylor's Tend-and-Befriend (`14.3-14.4`)**:
   - **SAM Axis (`Sympathetic-Adrenal-Medullary`)**: Instant epinephrine/norepinephrine `Fight-or-Flight` burst.
   - **HPA Axis (`Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal`)**: Sustained **Cortisol** release; chronic elevation damages hippocampal neurons, shortens **telomeres**, and suppresses **immune lymphocytes (`B, T, NK cells` -> Psychoneuroimmunology `PNI`)** (e.g., medical students' wounds heal `40%` slower during final exams, `Kiecolt-Glaser`).
   - **Selye's GAS**: `Stage 1 Alarm -> Stage 2 Resistance -> Stage 3 Exhaustion (illness/burnout)`.
   - **Shelley Taylor's `Tend-and-Befriend` (`14.3.3`)**: Especially in females (modulated by **oxytocin** + estrogen), stress triggers nurturing offspring (**tending**) and affiliating with social support networks (**befriending**) rather than solo fight-or-flight.
2. **Lazarus's Two-Stage Cognitive Appraisal (`14.4 Psych Matters`)**:
   - **Primary Appraisal**: *"Is this event irrelevant, benign/positive, or stressful (`Harm/Loss`, `Threat`, or `Challenge`)?"*
   - **Secondary Appraisal**: *"What coping resources and options do I have, and how effective will they be?"*
   - **Key Rule**: Reappraising a stressor from a paralyzing **Threat** (`vascular constriction`) to an energizing **Challenge** (`cardiac output boost`) transforms distress into **eustress**.
3. **Stress Vulnerability vs. Resilience Profiles (`14.2 & 14.5-14.7`)**:
   - **Maslach's 3 Dimensions of Job Burnout (`14.2.2`)**: (1) **Emotional Exhaustion**, (2) **Depersonalization / Cynicism** toward clients, (3) **Reduced Personal Accomplishment**.
   - **Type A Toxic Core (`Friedman & Rosenman`, `14.5`)**: Time urgency and competitiveness are harmless alone; **Chronic Cynical Hostility & Anger** is the toxic ingredient that doubles coronary heart disease risk.
   - **Kobasa's Hardiness (`3Cs`, `14.7`)**: **Challenge** (viewing change as growth, not threat), **Commitment** (deep engagement in work/life), and **Control** (internal locus of control).
   - **Seligman's Learned Optimism (`14.7.1`)**: Explaining setbacks as **Temporary** ("not permanent"), **Specific** ("not pervasive across my whole life"), and **External/Modifiable** ("strategy error, not personal defect").
4. **Zimbardo's Time Perspective Therapy (`TPT`) for PTSD (`14.1.3`)**:
   - PTSD traps survivors in a toxic `Past-Negative` trauma loop + `Present-Fatalistic` helplessness with a foreshortened `Future`. **TPT** systematically re-anchors `Past-Positive` identity, builds safe `Present-Hedonistic` joy, and constructs a hopeful, purposeful `Future`.

## A1 - Book Applications (Past Application)

### Case 1: Rosenhan's "Being Sane in Insane Places" & Diagnostic Labeling (`Ch 12.13 & Critical Thinking Applied`)

- **Problem**: Once a person is given a psychiatric label (`"schizophrenic"`), how does the clinical environment interpret their normal everyday behavior?
- **Method & Result**: David Rosenhan and 7 sane confederates gained admission to 12 psychiatric hospitals by reporting a single fake auditory hallucination (*"empty, hollow, thud"*), then immediately acted completely normal. Hospital staff **never detected their sanity** (discharging them after `7-52 days` as *"schizophrenia in remission"*), pathologized normal note-taking as *"writing behavior,"* and spent `< 7 minutes/day` interacting with patients-demonstrating how **diagnostic labels cause confirmation bias, stigma, and depersonalization**.

### Case 2: Derek's Depression & Why Mandatory CISD Debriefing Fails (`Ch 13 Opening Problem & Ch 14.8.6`)

- **Problem**: Should moderate depression or everyday grief (`"the worried well"`) be treated with pills alone, and should trauma survivors undergo mandatory single-session **Critical Incident Stress Debriefing (`CISD`)**?
- **Method & Result**: For Derek's depression, **CBT + Exercise** (or combined `CBT + SSRI` in severe cases) outperforms medication alone by teaching lifelong cognitive skills that prevent relapse (`13.11.1`). Meanwhile, controlled trials (`McNally et al., 14.8.6`) show mandatory immediate **CISD debriefing** after catastrophes **fails to prevent PTSD and can worsen trauma symptoms** by interfering with natural social coping and forcing premature emotional reliving (`Persistence`).

## E - Executable Steps (Execution)

1. **Run the 4-Indicator & Labeling Safety Screen**: Evaluate `Distress, Maladaptiveness, Irrationality, Unpredictability` across cultural context (`12.2 & 12.14`); protect the **"Worried Well" (`13.12`)** from unnecessary diagnostic labeling and overmedication while referring true clinical syndromes to licensed professionals.
2. **Match Evidence-Based Modality (`Ch 13 Matrix`)**: Apply **Graduated Exposure / Systematic Desensitization** to phobias, **ERP + CBT** to OCD, **CBT / REBT (`ABCDE`) + Positive Psychotherapy + Aerobic Exercise** (plus `SSRIs` if severe) to depression, and **Medication + Psychosocial Family/Skills Support** to bipolar/schizophrenia.
3. **Execute the 4-Part Stress Transformation Protocol (`Ch 14.8-14.10`)**:
   - **Appraisal & Cognitive Restructuring**: Shift Lazarus appraisal from `Threat` to `Challenge`, apply **Hardiness (`3Cs`)**, and reframe setbacks as `Temporary, Specific, and Modifiable`.
   - **Match Coping Strategy to Controllability**: Use **Problem-Focused Coping** for controllable stressors and **Emotion-Focused Coping** (`acceptance, Pennebaker expressive writing, mindfulness`) for uncontrollable losses.
   - **Rebalance ZTPI & Lifestyle Pillars**: Shift from `Past-Negative / Present-Fatalistic` to `Past-Positive + Future` (`TPT`), and lock in **Social Support (`Tend-and-Befriend`)**, **7.5-8 hr Sleep**, **Meditation**, and **Regular Aerobic Exercise** (`150+ min/wk`, which rivals antidepressants in mild-to-moderate depression and stimulates hippocampal neurogenesis, `14.9.4`).

## B - Boundary and Failure Modes (Boundary)

- **Failure Mode ("Medical Student's Disease" / Self-Diagnosis Trap, `Ch 12.2.3`)**: Reading DSM-5 symptom lists frequently triggers normal students to self-diagnose severe mental disorders because everyday anxieties and mood swings overlap with clinical criteria in *kind* but not in *severity, duration, or maladaptiveness*.
- **Failure Mode (Using Benzodiazepines or Avoidance as Primary Anxiety Treatment, `Ch 13.7.3`)**: Relying solely on anti-anxiety sedatives (`Xanax/Valium`) without **CBT/Exposure** prevents extinction learning, creates physical dependence, and triggers severe rebound anxiety upon stopping.
