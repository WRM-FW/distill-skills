# zimbardo-psych-8th

Whole-book executable psychological methodology router distilled from Philip G. Zimbardo, Robert L. Johnson, and Vivian McCann's flagship textbook *Psychology: Core Concepts* (8th Edition / 《津巴多普通心理学》第8版, 14 Chapters, 50 Core Concepts) via the Cangjie Skill RIA-TV++ pipeline.

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user asks to:

- Audit a psychological, media, or everyday claim using Zimbardo's **6 Critical Thinking Questions**, design a controlled **4-Step Empirical Study** (`Experiment` vs. `Correlational` vs. `Survey` vs. `Naturalistic Observation` vs. `Case Study`), or explain a behavior across **Psychology's 6 Main Perspectives** (`Biological`, `Cognitive`, `Behavioral`, `Whole-Person`, `Developmental`, `Sociocultural`).
- Analyze **Biopsychological, Neural, or Epigenetic Mechanisms** (`Natural Selection`, `Epigenetic Tagging`, `All-or-None Action Potential`, `Neurotransmitter Agonists/Antagonists`, `CNS/PNS Autonomic Balance`, `EEG/CT/PET/MRI/fMRI`, `3 Brain Layers`, `4 Cortical Lobes`, `Split-Brain Cerebral Lateralization`).
- Diagnose **Sensory Detection, Pain Modulation, or Perceptual Distortion** (`Transduction`, `Absolute Threshold`, `Weber's Law JND`, `Signal Detection Theory 2x2 Matrix`, `Gate-Control Pain Theory`, `Bottom-Up vs. Top-Down Processing`, `Gestalt Grouping Laws`, `Inattentional and Change Blindness`).
- Design a **Behavior Modification or Habit Change Protocol** (`Classical Conditioning Acquisition/Extinction/Counterconditioning`, `Operant Reinforcement Contingencies`, `FR/VR/FI/VI Schedules`, `Premack Principle`, `Token Economy`, `5-Step Operant Modification Checklist`, `Bandura Observational Learning`).
- Optimize **Memory Encoding, Study Strategies, or Eyewitness/Recall Reliability** (`3-Stage Memory Model`, `Working Memory 7 +/- 2 Chunks & 4 Components`, `Deep Semantic Processing`, `Encoding Specificity`, `Schacter's 7 Sins of Memory`, `Prospective Memory Cues`, `Method of Loci`).
- Debias **Judgment, Decision-Making, Problem-Solving, or Intelligence/Mindset Framing** (`Kahneman System 1 vs. System 2`, `Algorithms vs. Heuristics`, `Mental Set & Functional Fixedness`, `6 Cognitive Biases`, `Deviation IQ Mean 100 SD 15`, `Spearman/Cattell/Sternberg/Gardner`, `Dweck Growth Mindset`, `Stereotype Threat Interventions`).
- Evaluate **Lifespan Development, Parenting, Moral Reasoning, or Aging** (`Prenatal Teratogens`, `Harlow Contact Comfort`, `Ainsworth Strange Situation Attachment`, `Piaget's 4 Stages vs. Vygotsky Scaffolding`, `Erikson's 8 Psychosocial Stages`, `Adolescent Synaptic Pruning`, `Kohlberg Moral Stages vs. Bandura's 8 Moral Disengagement Mechanisms`).
- Structure **Sleep Hygiene, Circadian Reset, or Altered Consciousness Protocols** (`Conscious vs. Nonconscious Processing`, `Default Mode Network [DMN]`, `90-Minute 5-Stage NREM/REM Architecture`, `Sleep Debt`, `6 Sleep Disorders`, `Dream Theories`, `Hypnosis`, `Meditation`, `Psychoactive Drug Classes`).
- Diagnose **Motivation, Self-Control, or Emotion Regulation Challenges** (`McClelland nAch`, `Overjustification Effect`, `Kenrick-Maslow Evolutionary Hierarchy of Needs`, `Multiple-Systems Hunger & Set Point`, `Mischel Marshmallow Impulse Control`, `Ekman 7 Universal Emotions`, `Fast Amygdala vs. Slow Cortical Pathways`, `Yerkes-Dodson Inverted-U`, `Two-Factor Emotion Theory`, `Emotional Intelligence`).
- Profile **Personality Dispositions, Self-Narratives, or Zimbardo Time Perspective (`ZTPI`)** (`Big Five OCEAN Traits`, `5 ZTPI Time Zones & Optimal Balanced Profile`, `Freudian Defense Mechanisms`, `Rogerian Unconditional Positive Regard`, `Bandura Reciprocal Determinism`, `Rotter Internal/External Locus of Control`, `McAdams Redemptive vs. Contamination Narratives`).
- Deconstruct **Social Influence, Groupthink, Obedience, Bystander Inaction, Prejudice, or Institutional Evil (`Lucifer Effect`)** (`Social Roles & Scripts`, `Asch Conformity`, `Janis Groupthink`, `Milgram Obedience Levers`, `Latane & Darley 5-Step Bystander Protocol`, `Sternberg Triangular Love`, `Fundamental Attribution Error`, `Fiske Warmth-Competence Matrix`, `Stanford Prison Experiment`, `System Command & Heroic Defiance`).
- Screen **Psychological Distress, Match Psychotherapy vs. Biomedical Modalities, or Build Stress-Coping Resilience** (`4 Indicators of Abnormality`, `DSM-5 vs. NIMH RDoC`, `Rosenhan Labeling Trap`, `Insight/Behavioral/CBT/REBT/Positive Psychotherapy vs. Antipsychotics/Antidepressants/Mood Stabilizers/ECT/rTMS`, `Selye 3-Stage GAS vs. Taylor Tend-and-Befriend`, `Lazarus Primary/Secondary Appraisal`, `Problem- vs. Emotion-Focused Coping`, `Hardiness 3Cs`).

Do not use this skill when:

- The user presents an acute psychiatric crisis, active suicidal ideation, severe psychotic decompensation, or requests a formal medical diagnosis or prescription medication order (immediately provide crisis support resources and defer to a licensed psychiatrist, clinical psychologist, or physician).
- The user wants astrological readings, handwriting analysis (`graphology`), polygraph "lie detection" validation, or unverified self-help clichés (unless auditing and debunking them using Chapter 1 Critical Thinking rules).

## Core Principles

1. **Empirical Skepticism and 6-Question Critical Thinking (`Ch 1`)**: Never accept "common sense," anecdotes, or authority claims at face value; subject every behavioral claim to operational definition, falsifiability, control-group comparison, bias control (`single/double-blind`), and the **6 Critical Thinking Questions** (`Source`, `Reasonable vs. Extreme`, `Evidence`, `Bias Contamination`, `Logical Fallacies [especially Correlation != Causation]`, `Multiple Perspectives`).
2. **Six-Perspective Triangulation (`Ch 1, Table 1.3`)**: No single viewpoint owns the whole truth about human behavior (`Necker Cube metaphor`). Systematically triangulate complex problems across **Biological** (brain, genes, epigenetics, neurotransmitters), **Cognitive** (schemas, expectations, memory, heuristics), **Behavioral** (environmental stimuli, reinforcement contingencies), **Whole-Person** (psychodynamic, humanistic, traits/ZTPI), **Developmental** (lifespan heredity x environment), and **Sociocultural** (situational and systemic power) levels.
3. **Constructive Mind and Indirect Neural Reality (`Ch 2, 3, 5, 6, 8`)**: Neither sensation nor memory is a passive video recorder. Sensory receptors transduce physical energy into neural signals (`Ch 3`), perception actively constructs meaning via bottom-up feature detection and top-down expectations/schemas (`Ch 3`), and memory reconstructs the past across sensory, working (`7 +/- 2 chunks`), and long-term stores-making both perception and memory adaptive yet vulnerable to illusions, inattentional blindness, and the **7 Sins of Memory** (`Ch 5`).
4. **Gene-Environment-Epigenetic Co-Action (`Ch 2, 6, 7, 10, 12`)**: Reject "nature *versus* nurture" dichotomies. Innate evolutionary blueprints and polygenic dispositions (`temperament`, `IQ heritability`, `vulnerability via diathesis-stress`) continuously interact with environmental nurture (`attachment`, `enriched schooling`, `trauma`, `nutrition`), physically altering gene expression via **epigenetic tags** and reshaping neural circuitry via **synaptic plasticity and pruning**.
5. **The Power of the Situation, the System, and Heroic Agency (`Ch 10, 11, 14`)**: Avoid the **Fundamental Attribution Error** (blaming solely individual character for bad behavior or crediting only willpower for success). Diagnose the **3-Level Triad** (`Person [Dispositions/Time Perspective]` x `Situation [Roles, Norms, Majority Pressure, Authority, Diffusion of Responsibility]` x `System [Institutional Ideology, Chains of Command]`), and redesign situational cues, reinforcement contingencies, cognitive appraisals, and **Everyday Heroic Imagination** to produce lasting behavioral change.

## Steps

### Step 1: Formulate the Operational Question and Run the 6-Question Critical Thinking Audit

Before diagnosing a behavior or prescribing an intervention, anchor the problem using Chapter 1's scientific framework (`01-scientific-method-six-perspectives-critical-thinking.md`):

1. **Operationalize Variables**: Translate vague labels (e.g., "stressed," "unmotivated," "toxic team," "bad memory") into observable, measurable **Operational Definitions** (`stimulus conditions`, `frequency/duration of behavior`, `cognitive self-report`).
2. **Audit Assumptions via the 6 Critical Thinking Questions**: Check `Source Credibility`, `Reasonable vs. Extreme Claim`, `Empirical vs. Anecdotal Evidence`, `Bias Contamination (Confirmation/Expectancy/Emotional)`, `Logical Fallacies (Correlation-Causation / False Dichotomy)`, and `Multiple Perspectives`.

### Step 2: Triangulate Across the 6 Psychological Perspectives and Route to Capability Cards

Map the problem across Zimbardo's **6 Perspectives** and load the corresponding capability card(s) from `references/capabilities/`:

- **Biopsychological, Sensory, Consciousness & Health Layer**: Use `02-biopsychology-epigenetics-neuroscience-brain-layers.md`, `03-sensation-signal-detection-perceptual-organization.md`, `08-consciousness-circadian-sleep-architecture-states.md`, and `12-psychopathology-dsm5-biopsychosocial-therapies-stress-coping.md` to check sleep debt, circadian phase, sensory overload, autonomic/HPA activation (`GAS`), and neurotransmitter/epigenetic factors.
- **Behavioral, Cognitive, Learning & Memory Layer**: Use `04-behavioral-learning-conditioning-modification.md`, `05-memory-encoding-retrieval-seven-sins-mnemonics.md`, and `06-thinking-heuristics-biases-intelligence-mindset.md` to audit reinforcement schedules, punishment traps, working memory load (`7 +/- 2 chunks`), deep semantic encoding, heuristic biases, and fixed vs. growth mindset (`Dweck`).
- **Developmental, Motivational, Personality & Social-Systemic Layer**: Use `07-lifespan-development-attachment-piaget-erikson-moral.md`, `09-motivation-hierarchy-self-control-emotion-regulation.md`, `10-personality-big-five-ztpi-time-perspective-narratives.md`, and `11-social-psychology-situational-power-lucifer-effect.md` to evaluate developmental stage, intrinsic vs. extrinsic rewards (`overjustification`), ZTPI time perspective balance, social norms/roles, and institutional system design (`Lucifer Effect`).

Verify all mechanism analyses and behavioral prescriptions against these core textbook reference tables:

| Diagnostic / Intervention Dimension | Mechanism / Category | High-Efficacy Prescription / Rule (`Zimbardo 8th Ed.`) | Common Pitfall / Failure Mode to Avoid |

### Step 4: Execute Boundary, Ethics, and Clinical Scope Verification

1. **Research and Intervention Ethics Check (`Ch 1.9.2`)**: Verify `Informed Consent`, `Protection from Harm and Discomfort`, `Privacy/Confidentiality`, and `Full Post-Study Debriefing` whenever deception or behavioral experimentation is involved.
2. **Diagnostic Labeling and Cultural Context Check (`Ch 12.13-12.14`)**: Never equate eccentric or culturally normative behavior with psychopathology; require the **4 Indicators of Abnormality** (`Distress`, `Maladaptiveness`, `Irrationality`, `Unpredictability`) and guard against **Depersonalization via Diagnostic Labels (`Rosenhan Study`)**.
3. **Clinical Referral Gate (`Ch 1.1.3, Ch 12, Ch 13`)**: Clearly distinguish educational/applied psychological frameworks from licensed psychiatric or clinical care; refer severe, persistent, or life-threatening symptoms immediately to qualified professionals.

## Gotchas

- **Using Controlling Extrinsic Rewards on Intrinsically Motivated Tasks (`Overjustification Effect, Ch 9.2.1`)**: Paying people or giving tangible trophies for activities they already enjoy shifts their attribution from internal pleasure ("I do it because I love it") to external compliance ("I do it for the reward"), causing intrinsic motivation to collapse once rewards stop.
- **Confusing Negative Reinforcement with Punishment (`Ch 4.5.1`)**: **Negative reinforcement** *increases* a target behavior by removing an aversive stimulus (e.g., taking aspirin removes headache; buckling a seatbelt stops the buzzer), whereas **punishment** aims to *decrease* a behavior by adding an aversive stimulus (`positive punishment`) or removing a pleasant privilege (`negative punishment / omission training`).
- **Trusting "Learning Styles" (`VAK`) or "Left-Brain/Right-Brain" Typologies (`Ch 2 & Ch 4 Critical Thinking Applied`)**: Controlled empirical studies (`Pashler et al., Ch 4`) show that matching instruction to a student's self-reported Visual/Auditory/Kinesthetic preference produces **zero learning gain**; effective learning depends on **elaborative semantic processing, dual visual-verbal coding, and active retrieval** across both cerebral hemispheres.
- **Mandating Immediate Group Psychological Debriefing After Trauma (`Ch 14.8.6`)**: Forcing trauma survivors to recount vivid emotional details in single-session group debriefings (`CISD`) within 24-72 hours of a catastrophe disrupts natural psychological defenses and increases PTSD symptoms; provide practical safety/social support first and offer evidence-based **CBT or Zimbardo's Time Perspective Therapy (`TPT`)** when ready.
- **Blaming "Bad Apples" While Ignoring the "Bad Barrel" and "Bad Barrel-Makers" (`Ch 11.10-11.11`)**: In organizational failures, bullying, or ethical scandals, firing individuals without reforming role scripts, anonymity/deindividuation, diffused responsibility, authority pressure, and institutional reward systems guarantees that replacements will replicate the same destructive behavior.
