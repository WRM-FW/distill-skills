### 1. Structural Analysis (全书骨架与 26 章架构映射)

- **Book Metadata**: *Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning*, Fifth Edition, edited by G. Gregory Haff, PhD, CSCS,*D, FNSCA, and N. Travis Triplett, PhD, CSCS,*D, FNSCA (National Strength and Conditioning Association / Human Kinetics, 2027, 26 Chapters).
- **Core Proposition**: Maximizing athletic performance while minimizing injury and overtraining risk requires translating multi-disciplinary exercise science (neuromuscular physiology, biomechanics, bioenergetics, endocrinology, psychology, and sports nutrition) into individualized, valid performance testing, technically sound movement execution, periodized training prescription, medical/rehabilitation reconditioning, and legally defensible facility administration.

  2. **Module 2: Athlete-Specific Foundations: Age, Sex, Psychology, Nutrition, and Pharmacology (Chapters 7-12)**:

### 2. Interpretive Analysis (5th Edition 核心机制与方法论演进)

- **Impulse-Momentum as the Unifying Mechanical Law**: Across biomechanics (Ch 2), testing (Ch 14), resistance training (Ch 18), plyometrics (Ch 19), and speed/COD (Ch 20), the 5th Edition shifts from isolated "peak power" toward **Net Impulse (`F x dt = m x dv`)** and **Rate of Force Development (RFD)** within sport-specific time constraints (0-200 ms).
- **Autoregulated and Sub-Failure Loading (SRB & RIR)**: While retaining classic `%1RM` tables, Chapter 18 introduces **Set-Repetition Best (SRB)** and **Repetitions in Reserve (RIR / RPE)** to account for multi-set fatigue and avoid chronic training to failure, preserving rapid force production (RFD) and managing fatigue (`Fitness-Fatigue Paradigm`).
- **Phase-Specific Force-Velocity and Eccentric Overload**: Through Weightlifting Catching/Pulling/Pressing Derivatives (`Ch 16 & 18`, 30% to 140% 1RM) and **Accentuated Eccentric Loading (AEL, `Ch 17`, up to 105-120% 1RM)**, practitioners target exact regions of the force-velocity curve and overcome the concentric-eccentric strength deficit.
- **Holistic Athlete Health, Safety, and Recovery**: The 5th Edition integrates **RED-S / Energy Availability (`Ch 11`)**, **Trimester-Specific Pregnancy & Pelvic Floor Guidelines (`Ch 8`)**, **3-Step ABC Mental Health Triage (`Ch 9`)**, **Exertional Medical Conditions (`Ch 23`)**, and the **Overreaching-Overtraining Continuum (`Ch 24`)**.

### 3. Critical Analysis & Boundaries (适用边界与反例警示)

- **Load-Velocity (L-V) Profiling Boundary (`Ch 14`)**: Although barbell velocity tracking (VBT) is valid for monitoring training intent, predicting daily 1RM back squat from submaximal L-V regression equations systematically overestimates true 1RM and can lead to dangerous misprogramming.
- **Instantaneous Peak Power Fallacy (`Ch 2`)**: Force plates sampling at 1,000 Hz capture 1-ms instantaneous peak power over 1-2 mm of displacement; practitioners must evaluate mechanical work, propulsion duration, mean power, and net impulse (`mRSI = Jump Height / Time to Takeoff`) rather than chasing inflated 1-ms peak power numbers.
- **Professional Scope Boundaries (`Ch 9, 10, 11, 23`)**: S&C professionals do not diagnose mental illnesses (refer persistent >2-3 wk ABC distress to licensed counselors/psychologists), do not prescribe medical nutrition therapy or diagnose eating disorders (refer to RD/CSSD and team physician), and do not independently clear injured athletes during tissue inflammation/repair phases.
