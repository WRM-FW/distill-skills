# Capability 06: 7-Step Resistance Program Design, SRB, RIR, and Weightlifting Derivatives

## R - Source Anchor (Reading)

- **Source Location**: Chapter 18 (Program Design for Resistance Training, `Tables 18.1-18.15`, `Figures 18.1-18.11`).
- **Core Mechanism**:
  - **Six Principles of Anaerobic Exercise Prescription**: (1) **Specificity (SAID)**, (2) **Overload** (including velocity overload via lighter ballistic loads or supercompensation via a planned **deload week**, `Figure 18.1`), (3) **Progression**, (4) **Variation** (heavy/light days, exercise derivatives), (5) **Reversibility** (preventing involution via **retaining loads**), and (6) **Individualization** (~80-90% shared core structure, ~10-20% individualized exercises, 100% individualized loads).
  - **Seven-Step Program Design Sequence**:
    1. **Step 1: Needs Analysis** (Sport Evaluation [Movement, Physiological, Injury] + Athlete Assessment [Training Status `Table 18.1`, Force-Plate Diagnostic Profile `Figures 18.3-18.5`, Seasonal Goal `Table 18.2`])
    2. **Step 2: Exercise Selection** (Core [Structural/Power] vs. Assistance; Weightlifting Derivatives Spectrum `Figure 18.6 & Table 18.14`; Agonist-Antagonist Balance)
    3. **Step 3: Training Frequency** (`Tables 18.4-18.7`; Total-Body Push-Pull, Split Routines, or In-Season **Microdosing**)
    4. **Step 4: Exercise Order** (Power -> Nonpower Core -> Assistance; Upper/Lower; Push/Pull; Supersets/Compound Sets)
    5. **Step 5: Training Load and Repetitions** (`%1RM` `Tables 18.8-18.9`, `RPE/RIR` & **Set-Repetition Best [SRB]** `Table 18.10`, Updated RM Continuum `Figure 18.11`, Goal Assignments `Table 18.11`, 2-for-2 & 5% SRB Progression `Table 18.12`)
    6. **Step 6: Volume** (`Volume-Load` & `Volume-Load Displacement`, Sets/Reps by Training Status `Table 18.13`)
    7. **Step 7: Rest Periods** (`Table 18.15`: 2-5 min for Strength/Power; 2-3 min multijoint / 60-90 s single-joint for Hypertrophy; <=30 s for Muscular Endurance)

## I - Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

### 1. Step 1 & 2: Force-Plate Diagnostic Profiling & Weightlifting Derivatives (`Figures 18.3-18.6`, `Table 18.14`)

- **Force-Plate Diagnostic Decision Rule (`Figures 18.3-18.5`)**:
  - If an athlete has high CMJ height but average/poor **mRSI (`Jump Height / Time to Takeoff`)** due to prolonged takeoff duration (e.g., `1.0 s`), or poor **Rebound Jump height** despite fast GCT (`163 ms`), and only average **IMTP Peak Force / Relative Peak Force**, the primary bottleneck is **maximal and rapid force production**. Prescribe a **Maximal Strength block** first (which increases force and shortens time to takeoff), followed by a **Speed-Strength block**.
- **Weightlifting Catching, Pulling, and Overhead Pressing Derivatives Spectrum (`Table 18.14` & `Figure 18.6`)**:

| Derivative Category | Specific Exercises | Prescribed %1RM Range (of Power Clean/Snatch/Jerk) | Goal Repetitions per Set | Primary Force-Velocity Emphasis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High-Force Pulling Derivatives** | Mid-thigh pull, Countermovement shrug | **100-140% 1RM** | `4-6 reps` (short ROM preserves power across 6 reps) | **STRENGTH-Speed** (Maximal force & RFD) |
| **Moderate-to-High Force Pulling Derivatives** | Pull from the floor, Pull from the knee, Hang pull | **80-120% 1RM** | `2-6 reps` | **STRENGTH-Speed** to General Power |
| **Full/Hang Catching & Overhead Derivatives** | Power clean, Power snatch, Push press, Push jerk, Split jerk | **70-90% 1RM** | `2-5 reps` | **General Power** (Balanced Force x Velocity) |
| **Short-ROM Catching Derivatives** | Mid-thigh clean/snatch, Clean/snatch from knee, Hang clean/snatch | **60-80% 1RM** | `2-5 reps` | **General Power to SPEED-Strength** |
| **High-Velocity Pulling Derivatives** | Hang high pull | **45-60% 1RM** | `4-6 reps` | **SPEED-Strength** |
| **Maximal-Velocity Ballistic Derivatives** | Jump shrug (or Hex-bar jump / Jump squat at `0-30% 1RM`) | **30-45% 1RM** | `4-6 reps` | **SPEED-Strength** (Peak velocity) |

### 2. Step 3 & 4: Training Frequency and Exercise Order (`Tables 18.1, 18.4-18.7`)

- **Frequency by Status (`Table 18.4`)**: `Beginner` (`<2 mo`): **2-3 sessions/wk**; `Intermediate` (`2-6 mo`): **3-4 sessions/wk**; `Advanced` (`>=1 yr`): **4-7 sessions/wk**.
- **Frequency by Season (`Table 18.7`)**: `Off-season`: **4-6x/wk**; `Preseason`: **3-4x/wk**; `In-season`: **1-3x/wk** (or short **microdosing** bouts); `Postseason`: **0-3x/wk**.
- **Total-Body Push-Pull vs. Split Routines (`Tables 18.5-18.6`)**:
  - **3-Day Total-Body Push-Pull**: `Mon (Heavy Push)`, `Wed (Heavy Pull)`, `Fri (Light Mix @ 80% of Mon/Wed loads)`.
  - **4-Day Total-Body Push-Pull**: `Mon (Heavy Push)`, `Wed (Heavy Pull)`, `Thu (Light Push)`, `Sat (Light Pull)`.
  - **4-Day Upper/Lower Split**: `Mon (Heavy Lower)`, `Tue (Heavy Upper)`, `Thu (Light Lower @ 80-85%)`, `Fri (Light Upper @ 80-85%)`.

### 3. Steps 5, 6, 7: Load, Repetitions, Volume, Rest, and SRB/RIR (`Tables 18.8-18.15`)

| Training Goal | Load (%1RM) | Goal Repetitions (Novice / Interm / Adv) | Core Exercise Sets (Novice / Interm / Adv) | Rest Period Length (`Table 18.15`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Muscular Strength** | **>= 85% 1RM** (Core only; Assistance capped at `>=8RM`) | `<= 6` / `<= 6` / `<= 6` | `1-3` / `>= 3` / `>= 3` (up to ~8 sets/muscle group) | **2 to 5 minutes** |
| **Power: Single-Effort** | **80-90% 1RM** | `n/a` / `1-3` / `1-3` | `n/a` / `1-3` / `3-6` | **2 to 5 minutes** |
| **Power: Multiple-Effort** | **75-85% 1RM** | `n/a` / `3-6` / `3-6` | `n/a` / `1-3` / `3-6` | **2 to 5 minutes** |
| **Hypertrophy** | **67-80% 1RM** (or heavy `>80% 1RM` e.g., `10x3` in Adv `Fig 18.11`) | `8-12` / `6-12` / `3-12` | `1-3` / `>= 3` / `>= 3` (typically 3-6+ sets) | **2-3 min** (multijoint core lifts)<br>**60-90 s** (single-joint assistance) |
| **Muscular Endurance** | **<= 67% 1RM** | `12-15` / `12-15` / `12-25` | `1-3` / `>= 3` / `>= 3` (typically 2-3 sets) | **<= 30 seconds** |

- **%1RM-to-Repetition Lookup (`Table 18.8`)**: `100%=1`, `95%=2`, `93%=3`, `90%=4`, `87%=5`, `85%=6`, `83%=7`, `80%=8`, `77%=9`, `75%=10`, `70%=11`, `67%=12`, `65%=15`.
- **Set-Repetition Best (SRB) & RIR/RPE Autoregulation (`Table 18.10`)**:
  - Avoid chronic training to failure (`0 RIR`), which prolongs neuromuscular fatigue for 24-48+ hours without superior strength/power gains.
  - Prescribe working sets using **SRB 5% relative intensity bands** (percentage of the maximal load sustainable across the target multi-set scheme, e.g., `3x5` load is ~15% lower than `3x2` load):
    - **Very Heavy (`95-99% SRB`)**: `RPE 8.5-9.0` (`1 to 2 RIR`)
    - **Heavy (`90-94% SRB`)**: `RPE 7.5-8.0` (`2 to 3 RIR`)
    - **Moderate-Heavy (`85-89% SRB`)**: `RPE 6.5-7.0` (`3 to 4 RIR`)
    - **Moderate (`80-84% SRB`)**: `RPE 5.0-6.0` (`4 to 5 RIR`)
    - **Light-Moderate (`75-79% SRB`)**: `RPE 5.0` (`5 RIR`)
- **Load Progression (`Table 18.12`)**:
  - **2-for-2 Rule**: Add `+2.5-5 lb (1-2 kg)` upper / `+5-10 lb (2-4 kg)` lower for weaker/less-trained athletes; add `+5-10+ lb (2-4+ kg)` upper / `+10-15+ lb (4-7+ kg)` lower for stronger/more-trained athletes.
  - **SRB Weekly Progression**: Step up **+5% relative SRB intensity per week** across a 3-week loading block followed by a deload week (`Figure 18.1`).

## A1 - Book Applications (Past Application)

### Case 1: In-Season Collegiate Female Basketball Center (`Scenario A`, `Ch 18`)

- **Profile & Goal**: Advanced 20-yr-old center in-season; goal is **Strength/Power maintenance and development** across 3 total-body sessions/wk (`Mon: Heavy Push`, `Wed: Heavy Pull`, `Fri: Light Mix`).
- **Prescription**:
  - **Monday (Heavy Push)**: Push Jerk `3x3 @ 90 lb (75% est 1RM, 3 min rest)` -> Front Squat `3x4 @ 155 lb (85% 1RM, 3 min rest)` -> Incline Bench `3x4 @ 85 lb (85% 1RM, 3 min rest)` -> Triceps Pushdown `3x8 @ 40 lb (<8RM, 60-90 s rest)`.
  - **Wednesday (Heavy Pull)**: Hang Power Clean `3x3 @ 95 lb (75% est 1RM, 3 min rest)` -> RDL `3x4 @ 155 lb (3 min rest)` -> Seated Row `3x4 @ 120 lb (3 min rest)` -> DB Alternating Curl `3x8 @ 20 lb (60-90 s rest)`.
  - **Friday (Light Mix)**: Jump Shrug `3x3 @ 35 lb (~30% hang clean 1RM, 3 min rest)` -> Rear-Leg-Elevated Split Squat `3x4 @ 75 lb` -> Pull-Up `3x4 @ BW` -> Single-Leg Stiff-Legged Deadlift `3x4 @ 35 lb`.

## E - Executable Steps (Execution)

1. **Execute Step 1 Needs Analysis**: Output sport movement/physiological/injury analysis, athlete training status (`Table 18.1`), force-plate diagnostic bottleneck (`IMTP/CMJ/RJT`), and seasonal goal.
2. **Select & Order Exercises (Steps 2-4)**: Choose Core (`Power` including catching/pulling/pressing derivatives from `Table 18.14` + `Structural`) and `Assistance` exercises; set frequency (`Tables 18.4-18.7`) and sequence (`Power -> Core -> Assistance`).
3. **Prescribe Load, Volume, Rest, and Progression (Steps 5-7)**: Assign `%1RM` or `SRB/RIR` (`Tables 18.8-18.11`), `Sets x Reps` (`Table 18.13`), and `Rest Periods` (`Table 18.15`, including `2-3 min` for multijoint hypertrophy lifts), plus Heavy/Light day variation (`-15% to -20%` on light days).

## B - Boundary and Failure Modes (Boundary)

- **Failure Mode (Training Every Set to Momentary Muscular Failure)**: Consistently training at `0 RIR / RPE 10` degrades rapid force development (RFD), spikes muscle damage, and induces nonfunctional overreaching; keep most working sets at `1-3 RIR` (`85-95% SRB`).
- **Failure Mode (Using 60-90 s Rest on Heavy Multijoint Hypertrophy Lifts)**: In the 5th Edition (`Table 18.15`), multijoint core hypertrophy lifts (squats, deadlifts, bench press) require **2-3 minutes rest** to sustain volume-load, whereas **60-90 seconds rest** applies to single-joint assistance lifts.
