# Capability 12: Sports Nutrition, Ergogenics, Injury Rehab (DAPRE), Medical Emergencies, and Facility/Legal Administration

## R - Source Anchor (Reading)

- **Source Location**: Chapters 10-11 (Nutrition & RED-S, `Tables 10.1-10.15, 11.1-11.5`), Chapter 12 (Performance-Enhancing Substances, `Tables 12.1-12.4`, `Figures 12.1-12.5`), Chapter 23 (Rehabilitation, Reconditioning, and Medical Conditions), Chapter 25 (Facility Design, Layout, and Organization), and Chapter 26 (Facility Policies, Procedures, and Legal Issues).
- **Core Mechanism**:
  - **Professional Scope (`Ch 10, 11, 23, 26`)**: S&C professionals provide general nutrition education, execute reconditioning progressions in communication with the sports medicine team (Team Physician, Athletic Trainer, Physical Therapist), and enforce facility safety/EAPs; complex medical nutrition therapy, eating disorder treatment, and injury diagnosis must be referred to a **Board Certified Specialist in Sports Dietetics (RD/CSSD)** and **Team Physician**.
  - **Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S) & Low Energy Availability (LEA, `Ch 11`)**:
    - `Energy Availability (EA) = [Energy Intake (kcal) - Exercise Energy Expenditure (kcal)] / Fat-Free Mass (kg FFM)`
    - **Optimal EA**: `~45 kcal / kg FFM / day`
    - **Clinical RED-S Threshold**: `< 30 kcal / kg FFM / day` (triggers hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal suppression, functional hypothalamic amenorrhea, decreased RMR, bone stress injuries, iron deficiency, immune suppression, and impaired glycogen/protein synthesis).

### 1. Sports Nutrition, Hydration, and Body Composition Prescription (`Ch 10 & 11`)

| Nutritional Domain | NSCA 5th Edition Quantitative Prescription | Critical Operational Rules |

Sport drink: **20-30 mEq/L Na+ (460-690 mg/L)**, **2-5 mEq/L K+ (78-195 mg/L)**, **5-10% (ideal 6-8%) carb**, cooled to `10-15 C (50-59 F)`. **Youth**: `40 kg (88 lb)` child drinks `5 fl oz (148 mL)` q20min; `60 kg (132 lb)` adolescent drinks `9 fl oz (266 mL)` q20min.<br>- **Post-Exercise (<12 hr recovery)**: **1.5 L (51 fl oz) of fluid (with sodium) per 1 kg weight lost** (`0.7 L / 24 fl oz / 3 cups per lb lost`). | Never overdrink plain water beyond sweat losses (`post-workout weight > pre-workout weight`), which causes potentially fatal **exercise-associated hyponatremia** (`blood Na+ < 130 mmol/L`; severe cerebral edema/seizure risk `< 120-125 mmol/L`). |

- **DSM-V Eating Disorder Screening & Referral (`Ch 11`)**: Screen for **Anorexia Nervosa** (highest mortality rate of any psychiatric disorder; restricting vs. binge/purge subtypes), **Bulimia Nervosa** (binge + purge >=1x/wk for 3 months; normal BW, parotid swelling, tooth enamel erosion), **Binge-Eating Disorder** (binge >=1x/wk for 3 weeks without purging), **ARFID**, **Pica** (linked to iron-deficiency anemia), and **Rumination Disorder**. Refer immediately to the team physician and Eating Disorder multidisciplinary team.

### 2. Performance-Enhancing Substances and Dietary Supplements (`Ch 12`)

- **Banned Hormones, SARMs, and Peptides (`Tables 12.1-12.4`)**:
  - **Anabolic-Androgenic Steroids (AAS) & Prohormones**: Increase FFM/strength at supraphysiologic doses but cause testicular atrophy, suppressed LH/FSH/testosterone, lowered HDL-C, peliosis hepatis/liver damage, premature epiphyseal closure, tendon rupture, and mood/aggression disturbances. Prohormones (`androstenedione`, `DHEA`) fail to increase strength/muscle mass while lowering HDL-C and elevating estrogen (`Andro Project`).
  - **Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators (SARMs: `LGD-4033/Ligandrol`, `MK-2866/Ostarine/Enobosarm`, `RAD140/Testolone`, `GTx-007/Andarine`, `YK-11`) & Peptides (`MK-677/Ibutamoren`, `GHRPs`, `IGF-1`, `EPO`)**: WADA/NCAA prohibited at all times; no FDA-approved SARMs exist. Clinical/case data show dose-dependent lean mass gains accompanied by suppressed endogenous testosterone, SHBG, and HDL-C, plus acute **drug-induced liver injury and myocarditis** (`RAD140`), congestive heart failure signals (`MK-677`), and high rates (`48%`) of mislabeled/contaminated internet products (`212`).
- **Evidence-Based Legal Dietary Supplements (`Ch 12`)**:
  - **Creatine Monohydrate** (*most effective legal ergogenic aid*): Loading `20-25 g/day` (`0.3 g/kg/day`) for 5 days (or `~30 days` without loading) -> Maintenance `2 g/day` (`0.03 g/kg/day`). Saturates muscle at `150-160 mmol/kg dry weight` (`+20%`), increasing max strength/power (`5-20%`), repetitive sprint work, and FFM (`+0.5-1.8 kg [1-4 lb]`) with no renal/cramping harm; higher prolonged dosing also increases brain creatine (supporting concussion/TBI and sleep-deprivation resilience, `57, 72`).
  - **Caffeine**: `3 to 9 mg/kg` ingested `~60 minutes` prior to exercise (anhydrous tablets > coffee); improves aerobic endurance (`+28-43%` time to exhaustion) and trained power/sprint performance; no further benefit `>=9 mg/kg`.
  - **Beta-Alanine**: `4.0 to 6.4 g/day` (split into smaller doses to prevent transient **paresthesia** skin tingling) for `4-10 weeks`; rate-limiting precursor to intramuscular **carnosine** (`+58-73%`), improving MBC and high-intensity exercise bouts lasting **> 60 seconds**.
  - **Sodium Bicarbonate**: `0.3 g/kg` (`0.3-0.5 g/kg`) ingested `60-180 minutes` pre-exercise; extracellular buffer improving `60 s to 6 min` high-intensity bouts (test in practice due to high GI distress risk; `0.2 g/kg` is ineffective).
  - **HMB (`Beta-Hydroxy-Beta-Methylbutyrate`)**: `3 g/day` inhibits ubiquitin-proteasome protein degradation; effective in untrained beginners or during extreme catabolic/high-damage phases, minimal effect in steady-state trained athletes.
  - **L-Carnitine**: `2-3 g/day` for 3 weeks does *not* reliably increase fat oxidation, but enhances post-resistance recovery, reduces muscle damage, and upregulates skeletal muscle androgen receptors (`Kraemer et al.`).
  - **Arginine**: Oral doses (`6 g`) fail to increase nitric oxide, muscle blood flow, or exercise performance in healthy athletes; **not recommended**.

### 3. Injury Rehabilitation, Reconditioning (DAPRE), and Medical Conditions (`Ch 23`)

- **Three Tissue Healing Phases & Kinetic Chain Progression**:
  1. **Inflammation Phase** (`Days 0-3, up to 1+ wk`): Fibrin clot formation, edema, phagocytosis. **Rule**: **Zero active mechanical loading of injured tissue**; train uninjured limbs (`cross-education ~8-22%`, `Ch 5`) and non-impact cardiorespiratory fitness.
  2. **Fibroblastic Repair Phase** (`Day 2 to ~2 months`): Unaligned Type III collagen deposition. **Rule**: Begin pain-free **submaximal isometrics** -> **isokinetics** -> **low-load isotonics (`<50% 1RM`)** and neuromuscular/proprioceptive control.
  3. **Maturation-Remodeling Phase** (`Months to >1 year`): Type I collagen alignment along lines of stress (`Wolff's / Davis's Law`). **Rule**: Progress to **Closed & Open Kinetic Chain (CKC/OKC)** functional loading, eccentric overload, and sport-specific velocities using **DeLorme** (`50% -> 75% -> 100% 10RM`), **Oxford** (`100% -> 75% -> 50% 10RM`), or **Knight's 4-Set DAPRE** (`Set 1: 10 @ 50%`, `Set 2: 6 @ 75%`, `Set 3: Max reps @ 100%` -> adjust Set 4 and Next Session Set 3: `0-2 reps = -5 to -10 lb`; `3-4 reps = -0 to -5 lb / same`; `5-6 reps = same / +5 to +10 lb`; `7-10 reps = +5 to +10 lb / +5 to +15 lb`; `>=11 reps = +10 to +15 lb / +10 to +20 lb`).
- **Four Critical Medical Conditions (`5th Edition Ch 23 Addition`)**:
  - **Exertional Rhabdomyolysis**: Skeletal muscle breakdown releasing myoglobin and creatine kinase (`CK`) into blood (cola-colored urine, severe disproportionate muscle pain/swelling, acute kidney failure risk); triggered by novel, excessive high-volume/eccentric workouts after transition/time off, heat stress, and dehydration. **Prevention**: Gradual 2-4 week acclimatization after breaks; ban punitive high-rep conditioning.
  - **Sickle Cell Trait (SCT)**: Red blood cells sickle under hypoxemia, acidosis, hyperthermia, and dehydration during maximal sustained sprinting/conditioning, causing splenic/muscular ischemia and **exertional collapse with sickling (ECS)**. **Prevention**: Know athlete SCT status, allow longer work-to-rest intervals, eliminate timed all-out conditioning tests on day 1, ensure hydration, and stop immediately if cramping/weakness/slumping occurs.
  - **Exertional Heat Illness**: Progresses from Heat Cramps -> Heat Syncope -> Heat Exhaustion (`core temp < 40 C [104 F]`) -> **Exertional Heatstroke (`EHS: core temp >= 40 C [104 F]` + CNS dysfunction)**. **Golden Rule for EHS**: **Cool first, transport second** via immediate whole-body **cold-water immersion** until rectal temperature drops below `38.9 C (102 F)`.
  - **Cold Exposure**: Prevent hypothermia (`core temp < 35 C [95 F]`) and frostbite via windproof/moisture-wicking layering and windchill cutoffs.

### 4. Facility Design, Spacing, and Legal Administration (`Ch 25 & 26`)

- **Facility Spatial & Environmental Standards (`Ch 25`)**:
  - **Design Phases**: Predesign -> Design -> Construction -> Preoperation.
  - **Equipment Clearances**: **>=36 inches (91 cm)** between barbell ends, racks, and weight trees; **60 inches (152 cm)** for main walkways; **144 sq ft (`12 x 12 ft`)** per Olympic lifting platform (`8 x 8 ft` platform + `2 ft` perimeter); **130 sq ft** per power rack; **90 sq ft** per bench press; **40-50 sq ft** per athlete general space.
  - **Building Specs**: Ceiling height **12-14 ft (3.7-4.3 m)**; Floor load **>=100 lb/sq ft**; Lighting **75-100 foot-candles (807-1,076 lux)**; Temperature **68-78 F (20-26 C)**; Humidity **<=60%**; Air exchange **8-12/hr** (optimal `12-15/hr`); Sound **<=90 dB**; Mirrors **>=20 in (51 cm)** off floor and **>=6 in (15 cm)** from equipment; Ramp slope **12:1** (`1 ft run per 1 in rise`).
- **Supervision Ratios, Negligence Defense, and EAP (`Ch 26`)**:
  - **Coach-to-Athlete Supervision Ratios**: Junior High / Middle School **<= 1:10**; High School **<= 1:15**; College / Professional **<= 1:20**.
  - **Four Elements of Legal Negligence**: (1) Duty, (2) Breach of duty, (3) Proximate cause, (4) Damages.
  - **Emergency Action Plan (EAP)**: Written, posted at every venue, rehearsed at least annually by all staff (CSCS + current CPR/AED/First Aid), detailing (1) EMS activation, (2) emergency personnel roles, (3) communication chain, and (4) exact location of AED, splint kit, spine board, and cold-water immersion tub.

## A1 - Book Applications (Past Application)

### Case 1: Preventing Post-Break Exertional Rhabdomyolysis and Sickling Collapse (`Ch 23 & 26`)

- **Problem**: A collegiate team returns from a 3-week holiday break and is immediately subjected to a timed 300-yard shuttle test and 10 sets of 10 back squats with 60-s rest.
- **Method & Conclusion**: Abrupt high-volume eccentric loading and maximal glycolytic acidosis after inactivity are the primary triggers for exertional rhabdomyolysis and SCT collapse. Enforce a progressive 2-week transition acclimatization period, verify preparticipation medical clearance and SCT status, provide `1:10-1:20` supervision, and keep the venue EAP and cold-water tub ready.

## E - Executable Steps (Execution)

1. **For Nutrition & RED-S**: Compute `Cunningham RMR x PAL`, check `Energy Availability >= 45 kcal/kg FFM/day` (flag `<30`), prescribe sport-specific protein (`1.4-2.2+ g/kg`), pre/during/post carbs (`Tables 11.1-11.3`), and `1.5 L/kg` post-workout fluid replacement.
2. **For Supplements**: Recommend only verified, third-party-tested (`NSF Certified for Sport / Informed-Sport`) legal ergogenics (`Creatine`, `Caffeine`, `Beta-Alanine`, `Sodium Bicarbonate`, `EAAs/Protein`) and warn against SARMs/peptides/AAS.
3. **For Rehab & Facility Operations**: Match exercises to tissue healing phase (`Inflammation -> Repair -> Remodeling`) using `DAPRE`, enforce `36-in / 60-in` equipment clearances and `1:10 / 1:15 / 1:20` supervision ratios, and maintain an active `EAP` (`Cool first, transport second` for EHS).

## B - Boundary and Failure Modes (Boundary)

- **Failure Mode (Transporting an Exertional Heatstroke Athlete Before Cooling)**: Delaying cold-water immersion to wait for ambulance transport increases morbidity and mortality; always immerse onsite until rectal temperature is `<38.9 C (102 F)` before transport (`Ch 23`).
- **Failure Mode (Unverified Dietary Supplements)**: Up to 15-40%+ of over-the-counter supplements contain undeclared banned prohormones, SARMs, or stimulants (`Ch 12`); athletes are strictly liable under WADA/NCAA rules.
