# Capability 08: Aerobic Endurance Exercise Training Design

## R - Source Anchor (Reading)

- **Source Location**: Chapter 6 (Adaptations to Aerobic Endurance Training, `Tables 6.1-6.3`) and Chapter 18 (Aerobic Endurance Exercise Training, `Tables 18.1-18.5`).
- **Core Mechanism**:
  - **Three Physiological Determinants of Aerobic Performance (`Chapter 18`)**:
    1. **Maximal Aerobic Power (`VO2max`)**: Ceiling of oxygen uptake (`Fick Equation: VO2 = Cardiac Output x a-vO2 difference`).
    2. **Lactate Threshold (`LT` / `OBLA`)**: Fraction of `VO2max` sustainable without blood lactate accumulation; strongest predictor among athletes with similar `VO2max`.
    3. **Exercise Economy**: Oxygen cost (`VO2`) required to maintain a given submaximal velocity.
  - **Altitude Adjustments (`Table 6.3`)**: Acute exposure (>1,200 m) triggers hyperventilation (respiratory alkalosis -> renal HCO3- excretion) and elevated submaximal HR/cardiac output; chronic acclimatization stimulates erythropoietin (EPO), polycythemia, capillary density, and mitochondrial proliferation.

## I - Methodology Skeleton (Interpretation)

### 1. Five-Step Aerobic Endurance Design Process (`Chapter 18`)

- **Step 1: Exercise Mode**: Must match the sport's specific movement pattern (running, cycling, swimming, rowing) to induce peripheral muscular adaptations (capillary/mitochondrial density in recruited fibers).
- **Step 2: Training Frequency**: Governed by intensity, duration, training status, and sport season (always include **>= 1 recovery/active rest day per week**).
- **Step 3: Training Intensity Monitoring (`Tables 18.1-18.3`)**:
  - **Karvonen (Heart Rate Reserve) Method**:
    - `APMHR = 220 - Age`
    - `HRR = APMHR - RHR`
    - `Target Heart Rate (THR) = (HRR x Intensity%) + RHR`
  - **Percentage of Maximal Heart Rate (%MHR) Method**: `THR = APMHR x Intensity%`
  - **%VO2max vs. %HRR vs. %MHR Mapping (`Table 18.1`)**:
    - `50% VO2max = 50% HRR = 66% MHR`
    - `60% VO2max = 60% HRR = 74% MHR`
    - `70% VO2max = 70% HRR = 81% MHR`
    - `75% VO2max = 75% HRR = 85% MHR`
    - `80% VO2max = 80% HRR = 88% MHR`
    - `85% VO2max = 85% HRR = 92% MHR`
    - `90% VO2max = 90% HRR = 95% MHR`
  - **RPE (`Table 18.2`)**: Borg 6-20 scale (11 = Light, 13 = Somewhat hard, 15 = Hard, 17 = Very hard, 19 = Extremely hard) or 0-10 CR scale.
  - **METs (`Table 18.3`)**: `1 MET = 3.5 mL / kg / min`.
- **Step 4 & 5: Duration and Progression**: Frequency, intensity, or duration **must not increase by more than 10% per week**.

### 2. Five Aerobic Endurance Training Program Types (`Table 18.4`)

| Training Type | Weekly Frequency | Work Bout Duration | Prescribed Intensity | Primary Physiological Adaptation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long, Slow Distance (LSD)** | 1-2x/wk | Race distance or longer (~30-120 min) | ~70% VO2max (~80% MHR; conversational) | Cardiovascular/thermoregulatory function, mitochondrial oxidative capacity, fat utilization (glycogen sparing) |
| **Pace / Tempo** (Steady or Intermittent) | 1-2x/wk | ~20-30 min | At Lactate Threshold (at or slightly above race pace) | Elevates Lactate Threshold (`LT`/`OBLA`) and running economy at race pace |
| **Interval Training** | 1-2x/wk | 3-5 min bouts (min 30 s) with **1:1 W:R ratio** | Close to VO2max | Accumulates extended time at VO2max; increases VO2max and anaerobic metabolism |
| **Repetition (REPS) Training** | 1x/wk | 30-90 s bouts with **1:5 W:R ratio** (4-6x work duration rest) | Greater than VO2max (supramaximal) | Improves running speed, economy, glycolytic tolerance, and final race kick |
| **Fartlek Training** | 1x/wk | ~20-60 min continuous | Varies between LSD (~70% VO2max) and Pace/Tempo (LT) bursts | Challenges both aerobic and anaerobic systems; reduces monotony |

### 3. Seasonal Aerobic Periodization & Special Issues (`Table 18.5`)

- **Off-Season (Base Training)**: 5-6 sessions/wk | Long duration | Low-to-moderate intensity (build conditioning base).
- **Preseason**: 6-7 sessions/wk | Moderate-to-long duration | Moderate-to-high intensity (integrate all 5 training types).
- **In-Season (Competition)**: 5-6 sessions/wk (training + racing) | Short duration (training) / Race distance (racing) | Low intensity (training) / High intensity (racing).
- **Postseason (Active Rest)**: 3-5 sessions/wk | Short duration | Low intensity (non-specific cross-training recovery).
- **Tapering**: Systematic reduction of training duration/volume (while maintaining intensity and moderate frequency) over 1-3 weeks pre-competition to restore glycogen and peak performance.

## A1 - Book Applications (Past Application)

### Case 1: Karvonen Target Heart Rate Range for a 30-Year-Old Athlete (`Table 18.1`)

- **Inputs**: Age = 30 yr, RHR = 60 beats/min, prescribed intensity = 60-70% HRR.
- **Calculation**: `APMHR = 220 - 30 = 190 bpm`; `HRR = 190 - 60 = 130 bpm`.
  - Low THR = `(130 x 0.60) + 60 = 138 bpm` (`23 beats per 10 s`).
  - High THR = `(130 x 0.70) + 60 = 151 bpm` (`25 beats per 10 s`).

## E - Executable Steps (Execution)

1. **Calculate Target Heart Rate Zones**: Compute both Karvonen (`HRR`) and `%MHR` ranges using `Table 18.1`.
2. **Select Weekly Mix of the 5 Modes**: Combine LSD, Pace/Tempo, Interval (1:1), Repetition (1:5), and Fartlek according to the athlete's event and season (`Tables 18.4-18.5`).
3. **Enforce Progression & Recovery Guardrails**: Include >=1 rest day/week, cap weekly volume/duration/intensity increases at **<=10%**, and schedule a pre-race taper.

## B - Boundary and Failure Modes (Boundary)

- **Failure Mode (Running LSD Faster Than ~70% VO2max)**: Turning LSD days into moderate-hard runs depletes muscle glycogen without reaching LT/VO2max stimulus, causing chronic fatigue on Interval/Tempo days.
- **Failure Mode (Starting Interval/Repetition Training Without an Aerobic Base)**: High-stress VO2max intervals and >VO2max repetitions require a solid off-season LSD/base foundation first (`Chapter 18`).
